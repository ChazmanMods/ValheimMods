# Sword of the Ancients Audio Review

## Generated weapon sounds

- `00_Previous_User_Hum.wav`: the supplied hum used before version 0.4.0, included only for comparison.
- `01_Idle_Hum.wav`: the new five-second continuous deployed loop.
- `02_Deploy_Start.wav`: played when the weapon is drawn.
- `03_Retract_Stop.wav`: played when the weapon is put away.
- `04_Swing_Whoosh_01.wav` through `06_Swing_Whoosh_03.wav`: randomized when Valheim starts the weapon trail during an attack.

## Original clash source

`Lightning_Clash_Source_01.wav` through `Lightning_Clash_Source_05.wav` are WAV extractions of Valheim's randomized `Player_Weapons_Enchant_Lightning_Impact` clips. These remain unchanged in the mod's contact effect and supplied the electrical source material for the generated sounds.

The complete in-game clash is layered: Valheim combines one of these electrical clips with its normal sword-hit and overlay effects.
