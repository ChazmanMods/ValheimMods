## Items

- Arcane Warrior Cowl
- Arcane Warrior Vestments
- Arcane Warrior Legwraps
- Sword of the Ancients

The three armor pieces use Valheim's native Fenris skinning and the Jedi garment textures authored for this mod: a cream wrap tunic and pants, brown leather boots, a deep-mahogany Fenris cowl, and a matching native Ashen cape. They form the Arcane Warrior set and grant **Quiet Current**, reducing movement and combat stamina costs while modestly increasing movement speed.

## Crafting

All items are crafted and upgraded at the Galdr Table. The armor uses Linen Thread, Blue Jute, Soft Tissue, and Refined Eitr. The sword uses Yggdrasil Wood, Silver, Black Cores, and Refined Eitr.

The Sword of the Ancients is a one-handed energy sword using Mistwalker's stance, primary combo, secondary attack, and handling. Quality levels deliver exactly 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of its final 250 slash, 105 spirit, and 45 lightning damage. Version 0.7.1 restores the full supplied idle-hum loop, uses Fenris armor materials with the native Fenris meshes, plays the swing-plus-lightning impact combination on perfect parries, and delays the actual unequip until its single shutdown recording finishes.
