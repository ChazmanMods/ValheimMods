## Koi models and textures

Chazman created the koi models and textures included in this package, as confirmed for this release. They are original project assets, not native Valheim fish models.

## Native Valheim assets

Native splash effects and supporting game assets are referenced from the installed Valheim game at runtime. They are not redistributed as extracted assets. Valheim and its original assets belong to their respective owners. This is an unofficial community mod.

## Package artwork

The WaterGardens oval medallion icon is AI-generated artwork selected by Chazman and shared with the parent mod.

## Dependencies

This add-on requires ArcaneDecor:WaterGardens, BepInEx and Jötunn. Dependency assemblies and WaterGardens audio are not bundled in this add-on; their respective credits and terms remain with those projects.
