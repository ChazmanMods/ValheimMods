# 0.2.2 — Initial Thunderstore release

- Introduces ArcaneDecor:WaterGardens:KoiFish as an optional WaterGardens add-on.
- Adds Orange Koi, Golden Koi and Kohaku with individual counts and shared fish-size controls per pond.
- Includes smooth wandering, staggered surface visits, gentle feeding and player avoidance.
- Adds craftable Koi Food and placeable food supplies.
- Preserves settings and food IDs from the private WaterGardens_Koi builds.
