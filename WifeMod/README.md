# Wife Companion

A BepInEx/Jotunn add-on for Offline Companions using Valheim's female player model.

## Use

- Press **Left Shift+W** to spawn a companion.
- Interact to toggle follow/stay.
- Tap interact to open Offline Companions' inventory; hold interact to open its radial menu.
- Press **Left Shift+P** near your wife to check pregnancy status and days remaining.
- Use an inventory item on the companion to transfer it into her inventory.
- The companion consumes carried cookable ingredients at nearby cooking stations and carried fuel at nearby fireplaces.
- Interact with a bed while your adult, non-pregnant wife is nearby to begin the configurable fade-to-black pregnancy sequence. Marriage/ownership is the only relationship requirement.
- Pregnancy chance and gestation length are configurable; birth occurs after the configured number of in-game days.
- Wife scale defaults to `0.82` and can be adjusted under the Appearance section of the generated config.
- Wife Companion suppresses Offline Companions' autonomous bed claiming during the custom scene and forces a complete wake afterward.
- On the Dragon bed, the wife uses a dedicated side-by-side attachment anchor; the bed rocks vertically for five seconds before fading.
- Sequence text copies Valheim's live dream-description font, material, style, size, and cyan-blue color.

Wife and child are spawned through Offline Companions' manager so ownership, persistence, respawn, and appearance are registered correctly.
