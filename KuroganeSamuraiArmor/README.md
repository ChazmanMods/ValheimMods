## Items

- Kurogane Kabuto: Protects your thoughts. Not your choices.
- Kurogane Menpō Kabuto: For when your face wants plausible deniability. Includes nine selectable mask styles.
- Kurogane Dō-Maru: For when bare-chested bravery stops working.
- Kurogane Haidate: Keeps your knees from filing complaints.
- Kurogane Jinbaori: Dramatic billowing sold separately. Grants Feather Fall while equipped.

## Crafting

All five pieces are crafted at a level 1 Forge. Upgrades use Forge level 2 for quality 2, Forge level 3 for quality 3, and Forge level 4 for quality 4.

- Kurogane Kabuto: Silver, Iron, Leather Scraps, Deer Hide
- Kurogane Menpō Kabuto: Silver, Iron, Leather Scraps, Deer Hide
- Kurogane Dō-Maru: Silver, Iron, Leather Scraps, Deer Hide
- Kurogane Haidate: Silver, Iron, Leather Scraps, Deer Hide
- Kurogane Jinbaori: Silver, Iron, Leather Scraps, Deer Hide

Upgrades use Silver, Iron, and Leather Scraps.

## Set Bonus

The Kurogane Jinbaori grants Feather Fall while equipped. The full set grants Dragon and Tiger, including stamina bonuses and movement speed.

## Notes

This build uses Jotunn to clone the vanilla Flametal helmet, chest, and legs at runtime, keeping the Flametal helmet horns. The Kurogane Menpō Kabuto swaps in a bundled custom masked helmet visual while keeping the vanilla item physics and networking. In vanilla crafting, use the Style control to choose one of nine mask finishes. With AAA Crafting installed, choose one of the nine numbered Menpo recipe tiles instead. The selected finish is stored on the crafted helmet and synchronized when equipped. The cloak clones the Feather Cape to reduce overlap with the Flametal shoulders and to match the Feather Fall behavior. The wearable pieces keep Valheim's native mesh materials with a Kurogane tint so the UVs stay clean in-game.

The nine mask selector icons are exact crops from the supplied Samurai mask artwork.
