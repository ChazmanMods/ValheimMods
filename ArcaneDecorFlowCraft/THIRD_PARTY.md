# Asset credits

## Supplied plumbing models

The copper straight pipe, elbow, intake and Surtling powered pipe models and their base-colour textures were supplied by Chazman as AW_Copper_Pipe_2m.zip, AW_Copper_Elbow_90.zip, AW_Copper_Intake.zip and AW_Copper_Pipe_Surtling.zip. ArcaneDecor: FlowCraft scales these meshes for the requested lengths. Hammer icons are renders of those models. No additional third-party origin or licence is inferred from the supplied archives.


## Supplied dragon fountain

AD_Dragon_Spout_001.fbx and its base-colour texture were supplied by Chazman in AD_Dragon_Spout_001.zip, with the attached dragon-head image as visual reference. FlowCraft uses the supplied model at two scales and renders the model for its hammer icon. No external licence or origin is inferred.
